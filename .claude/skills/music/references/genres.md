# Genre → BPM / key / instrumentation cheat-sheet

Use as a starting point, then tune to the brief. BPM ranges are typical, not
rules. "Key" suggests a mood default — override for the lyric's emotion.

| Genre | BPM | Typical key/mood | Core instrumentation | Vocal feel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **City Pop** | 95–120 | Major 7ths, lush | Electric piano, slap bass, clean guitar, brass stabs, gated drums | Smooth, breezy |
| **Synthwave / 80s** | 100–120 | Minor, neon | Analog synths, arpeggios, gated reverb drums, FM bass | Airy, reverb-heavy |
| **J-Pop** | 120–145 | Bright major | Piano, bright synths, tight drums, layered vocals | Energetic, clean |
| **Lo-fi Hip-Hop** | 70–90 | Jazzy minor 7ths | Dusty drums, Rhodes, vinyl crackle, mellow bass | Often instrumental |
| **EDM / Future Bass** | 128–150 | Big major drops | Supersaws, sidechained pads, 808s, risers | Chopped/pitched |
| **Acoustic Ballad** | 60–80 | Emotional major/minor | Acoustic guitar/piano, strings, soft brushes | Intimate, dynamic |
| **Rock / Pop-Punk** | 140–180 | Power chords | Distorted guitars, driving bass, live drums | Raw, anthemic |
| **R&B / Neo-Soul** | 60–95 | Extended jazz chords | Rhodes, sub bass, finger snaps, smooth pads | Soulful, runs |
| **Funk** | 100–120 | Dominant 7ths | Slap bass, wah guitar, horn section, tight kit | Punchy, rhythmic |
| **Ambient / BGM** | 60–90 | Drifting, modal | Pads, textures, soft piano, field recordings | Usually none |
| **Drum & Bass** | 165–180 | Dark minor | Breakbeats, Reese bass, atmospheric pads | Sparse/chopped |
| **Trap** | 130–160 (half-time feel) | Dark minor | 808 bass, hi-hat rolls, sparse keys | Auto-tuned |

## Quick selection heuristics

- **"ノリの良い / アップテンポ"** → push to the upper BPM of the genre, major key.
- **"エモい / 切ない"** → lower BPM, minor or major-7th colour, leave space.
- **"おしゃれ / chill"** → city pop, lo-fi, or neo-soul; jazzy chords, soft drums.
- **"激しい / かっこいい"** → rock, DnB, trap; distortion or hard 808s.
- **BGM / 作業用** → ambient or lo-fi, instrumental, no strong hook needed.

## Vocal descriptors (for the style prompt)

`male vocal`, `female vocal`, `duet`, `choir`, `whispered`, `powerful belt`,
`soft airy`, `rap`, `spoken word`, `vocoder`, `instrumental` (no vocals).
